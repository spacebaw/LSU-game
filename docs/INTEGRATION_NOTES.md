Implementers: if you discover that ARCHITECTURE.md or your brief is ambiguous, wrong, or missing

## data.js

- **Shape:** `BSU.data` is deep-frozen (1,956 nodes, no functions except `selfTest`, no typed arrays). Load it right after `contract.js`; it reads nothing else.
- **Unused effect keys are the ARCH §4.1 zero values** (`0`, `false`, `''`, `[]`, `{value:0, radius:0}`, `{mult:0, radius:0, stacksTo:0}`). A `mult` of 0 means *unused*, not ×0: consumers must gate on `radius > 0` (mosquito, heat, windShield, happiness, noise, power, water) and on `research.kind !== ''`, `ecologyDecayMult > 0`, `coneDays > 0`.
- **Two ARCH §4.2 calendar keys do not parse with `BSU.dateToDay`:** `'Mar 25'` (`riverTicker`) and `'Dec 11'` (`break`). Both strings are also in `params.weather` (`highWater.tickerDate`, `semesterDates.break`, `mosq.coldStart`), and `dateToDay` (frozen) accepts day-of-month 1–10 only. `data.calendar.dates` keeps them verbatim; `data.selfTest` tolerates exactly those two. **weather.js** (the `calendar:date` emitter) needs a rule for them; note `BSU.dayParts` already puts the break at Dec 1–Jan 4 (yd 110–119, 0–3), so `'Dec 11'` most plausibly means "the day after Dec 10" and `'Mar 25'` has no 10-day-month equivalent (suggest firing it on the last spring day before Apr 1, i.e. Mar 10). Nothing in data.js depends on the answer.
- **`students.last` has 55 names**, not the 54 the brief counts; GDD §14.1 lists 55 (Boudreaux … Toups) and the GDD wins. `firstCajun` 56, `firstModern` 24, `nicknames` 16, `majors` 14, `hometowns` 18 match the brief.
- **`surge_barrier.wr = 5`** (GDD §0.3) although it is a `drag` row: it produces a Building struct and takes wind damage. Other drag/paint/upgrade rows are `wr: 0` except `boardwalk` (2, flavor).
- **`allowMarsh`** is `true` for every row except `road` (GDD: "not on Marsh or Preserve"). For drag/paint rows it is informational; `placeRule` is the rule.
- **`pump.needsPower = true`** (decision in the brief): buildings can treat it as an ordinary ⚡ consumer, and it is in the campus core for the ring test.
- **Keys:** `data.keys` has one entry per `{key, ctrl, shift}` triple (45 entries; `ui.selfTest` may assert uniqueness). The toast-capture layer and the held pan keys collide with `n` (Season) and `w` (Water overlay), so they live in `data.toastKeys = {yes: ['y','Enter'], no: ['n','Escape']}` and `data.panKeys = {up: ['w','ArrowUp'], down: ['s','ArrowDown'], left: ['a','ArrowLeft'], right: ['d','ArrowRight']}`. `a`, `s`, `d` and the four arrows also appear in `keys` as `panLeft/panDown/panRight/panUp`; `Enter` appears only in `toastKeys`. Key names are `KeyboardEvent.key` values; the debug key is stored as `` '`' `` (ui should match `event.code === 'Backquote'`). `Shift+1…9` are actions `pick1`…`pick9`; speeds are `speed1`, `speed2`, `speed4`.
- **`objectives['8'].cappedText`** holds the Aug-5-missed variant; the matching deadline text (`'by Jan 10'`) is `params.progress.goals.deadline8b`. `objectives[id].teaches` is the GDD "Teaches / unlocks" column, sometimes longer than a card line; `text` is the ≤ 12-word card line.
- **`boardCards[].cost`** is the amount spent (positive) — `marshGrant` is `-300000` (a +$300k grant). The Homecoming Budget tiers are `params.econ.boardCards.homecomingBudget.costs`.
- **Extra tables beyond ARCH §4.2** (all frozen, all documented here): `data.tickerKindList` (the 7 valid ticker kinds), `data.toastKeys`, `data.panKeys`.
- **Missing from params (used local constants):** none. Every number in a row is transcribed from GDD §0.3; where params also carries the number (pump 15 tile-ft, generator 3 fuel days, research bases, rain totals, habitat radius) the unit test asserts they agree.
- **File size:** 78.5 KB against the brief's 60 KB guideline; 43 rows × every schema key plus the verbatim text tables is the floor. Definition time 3 ms; `selfTest` 2 ms.

## terrain.js

- **Shape:** `BSU.terrain` per ARCH §5.2 + the brief's extras (`template`, `walkClassOf`, `landRoute`, `subsidenceRate`, `plantVeg`, `removeVeg`, `isDisturbedMarsh`, `reachableHigh5`). Also exported: `typeOf(elev, flags)` (the pure §3.3 rule) and `_attempt(state, R, tpl)` (one generation attempt; tests use it for failure tallies and to force the template). Injectables: `M._deps.emit` (§10.6) plus `M._deps.hydro/buildings/wildlife` (getter functions returning the live modules; selfTest swaps all four).
- **What terrain calls in other modules (guarded; a missing function falls back):** `hydro.seedInitial(state)` at the end of every generation attempt (fallback: the §3.3 rule locally with a tile-hash marsh depth, `params.hydro.initial`); `hydro.surfaceAt/stageAt(state, i)` for boardwalk/bridge walk classes (non-finite answers fall back to `elev + depth` / 0); `hydro.drainsTo` in `tileAt`; `hydro.markLeveeChange(state, i)` after a sandbag expiry; `wildlife.nutriaTraps(state, i)` for instant debris clearing; `buildings.applySubsidence(state, id, maxFtOverFootprint)` once per affected building on Jan 1. Pilings and pump dewatering are read from the tree (`state.buildings[owner].pilings`, `type === 'pump' && built >= 1 && powered !== false && !ruin`), and the crossing footprints (`practice_field/stadium/parking` → walk 2) from `state.buildings[owner].type`, so the 4 Hz `rewalk(i)` path never makes a cross-module call.
- **Numbers the GDD states but `params` does not carry (local consts, file header lists them):** elev clamp −4…14; DESIRE_WORN clear threshold 20 (hysteresis; set at `agents.wearThreshold` 40); veg growth days oak/cypress `[oakGrowDays/2, oakGrowDays]` = [120, 240], palmetto [60, 120], azalea `[storm.azaleaRegrowDays, ×2]` = [30, 60]; the lake bowl (an elongated depression: lake radii ×2.5 E–W, ×5 N–S, −1.5·(1−r) ft, −0.9 at the rim); basin noise = 0.44 × the z-score (over the backswamp north of row 50, lake excluded) of `octave(scale 11) + 0.35·octave(scale 5.5)`, plus a north lift of +0.35 ft at row 0 tapering to 0 at row 40 (the swamp deepens toward the Gulf; needed so G6's Marsh runs survive at the §3.3 Wet share); chenier profile `rim + (crest−rim)·cos(π/2·r^1.5)` (flat crown, steep rim → ~10 High / ~35 Dry per chenier as §3.4 says; a plain cosine gives too few Dry/High for G7); cheniers may overlap earlier ones by ≤ 35 % of their tiles (ridge complexes; the GDD never says disjoint) and stay off the lake's marsh apron (tx ≤ 17, rows ≥ 34); the three random bayou control points are drawn in fixed bands ({26–32}×{34–42}, {36–42}×{26–36}, {44–48}×{18–26}) so the bayou hugs the ridge foot and enters the lake from the north-east above row 51 (both are what G6/G7 need; every point is also capped at `bank(ty) − 12`).
- **Geometry decisions:** river = `tx > bank(ty)` (the GDD's "tx ≥ 58 + 2·sin" leaves one undefined column when the sine is fractional; `bank` is the last land column). The bayou is carved as a disk of radius `bayou.width/2` around Catmull-Rom samples (2–3 tiles per cross-section, ~150 tiles ≈ 3.6 %); its 8-neighbours become 0.5-ft Marsh shoulders; the cove floor and rim are protected from the carve and the shoulders. Mouth = the rim tile nearest `(cx−2, cy+2)` plus its two nearest rim neighbours (`mouth[1]` is the centre). `landingShoulder` = the shoulder tile 8-adjacent to the landing nearest `mouth[1]` (falls back to any land neighbour). G1b lowering does exactly the two GDD steps (strays outside rows 5–14, then rows 5/14 at d ≥ 9); ~4 % of attempts still count 111–112 and re-roll. G8 is enforced during the carve (a would-be bayou tile ≥ 5 ft aborts the attempt) and re-checked post hoc (bayou flags/elev, landing within 2 of the mouth).
- **Guarantee readings:** G3 measures `landRoute` from the Founders' front tile `(founders.tx+1, founders.ty+3)` (the skipTutorial start, ARCH §9.4) to `plot.landingShoulder`, steps ≤ 16, cove tiles ≥ 3. `landRoute` is a Dijkstra with cost 64 per step − 1 per cove tile: the shortest route in steps, tie-broken toward the cove, so the tutorial path really gets wet (typically 14 steps, 5–7 cove tiles). G5's "separated by the Bayou" = none of the chenier's tiles is in `reachableDryHigh`. G6 counts columns `tx ≤ bank0 − 14`. Distribution counts DRAINED with Marsh. Single attempts pass ~55 % of the time (~5 ms each); over seeds 1–20 the template was never needed and `gen` averages ~9 ms (worst ~28 ms).
- **Template:** `template()` = fixed bayou start 30, fixed controls `[38,29],[28,38],[20,46]`, 7 fixed cheniers, an analytic basin relief (three crossed sinusoids through the same normalization), zero ridge noise, veg from a tile hash. It passes the guarantees (Marsh 40.4 / Wet 28.2 / Dry 12.1 / High 4.0).
- **Resulting distribution across seeds:** Water 11.8, Bayou 3.4–3.9, Marsh 37.5–41.5, Wet 27–30, Dry 12–14, High 3.2–4.5 (all inside ±3 of §3.3).
- **Relay rules:** `touch(i, 'crest')` also rewalks (a bare levee berm is class 2). `classify(state, i)` emits `tile:changed{type}` only on change; `classify(state)` (all) and `reset` reclassify silently. Daily work is three flat 4,096 scans (≈ 15 µs) instead of hot lists. `reset(state, fresh)` ignores `fresh` (nothing here draws `rng.sim`) and leaves an ungenerated tree alone (it keeps reading as all Open Water). `tick` does nothing on an ungenerated tree.
- **For progress/buildings/hydro:** set DEBRIS through `terrain.setFlag(state, i, BSU.FLAG.DEBRIS, true)`; write `surface` only through `terrain.setSurface`; report every other tile write through `terrain.touch(state, i, what)` (never from inside a `tile:changed` listener). Pond cut / grading / regrade go through `terrain.setElev` (clamped, reclassified, rewalked, touched).

## sprites.js

- **Painter contract (for `sprites_buildings.js` / `sprites_entities.js`):** `registerPainter(family, fn)` with `fn(ctx, spec) → {w, h, ox, oy}` in **1× units**. The core hands the painter a fresh context on an *unsized* canvas; the painter MUST call `BSU.sprites.begin(ctx, w, h, spec.zoom)` first (sizes the canvas to `w·zoom × h·zoom`, ≥ 1×1, smoothing off) and should draw through `const P = BSU.sprites.pen(ctx, spec.zoom)` (`rect/px/hline/vline/line/dither/diamond/ellipse/ellipseTest/glyph/text`, all in 1× coordinates → S×S blocks), which is what makes the same painter produce the 2× atlas (Tier 2 #9 is implemented and enabled: `BSU.sprites.ZOOM2 = true`; a 2× entry is built lazily on the first `get(id, v, f, 2)`). The core multiplies `ox/oy/w/h` by the zoom. A painter may set `spec.frames = n` (and `spec.framesPerVariant = true` when the count depends on the variant, e.g. NIGHT = 4) on its first call; the core stores it (`frames(id)` / `frames(id, variant)`). A painter may return `{canvas, sx, sy, sw, sh}` (device px) to hand back a sub-rect of a sheet it caches itself (per-look agent sheets); such entries are not counted by `memoryMB()`, so the owner should count them (or expose a `sheetBytes` for render). `spec = {id, family, sub, variant, frame, zoom, row, rot, seed, frames}` with `seed = hash(strHash(id), variant)`.
- **Family precedence (amends the brief's resolver):** an id that has its own registered family wins over the catalog lookup. `levee`, `floodwall`, `canal`, `cypress`, `azalea` are both catalog ids and terrain/tree families; `get('levee', mask)` is the berm tile, `get('cypress', stage|autumn<<2)` the tree. The building look of those rows (the palette icon) is `icon:<id>` (sprites_buildings brief). `dorm:r` → family `building`, `spec.rot = 1`, as the buildings brief asks. Re-registering a family replaces the painter (last wins) and drops that family's cached entries — the entities file may take over `oak/cypress/palmetto/azalea`; the painters here already produce the entities brief's sizes/variant packing (`oak` 8×14/40×36/96×56, `cypress` 6×16/16×48/24×80 with `autumn = 4`, `palmetto` 12×10/18×16/24×20, `azalea` 10×6 (stage 0) / 16×10 with `bloom = 8`; `treeVariant(stage, autumn, bloom)` is defined here), so nothing blocks if it does not.
- **Frame table:** the core carries the entities brief's frame totals as defaults (`FRAMES`: `agent` 16, `agent:idle` 8, `gator` 6, …) so `frames()` and frame wrapping are right even before the split files load; decal frames come from `data.decals`; `thibodeaux` 2.
- **Decal sizes are `data.decals`' sizes**, not the brief's prose (e.g. `tarp` 24×12, `cars` 8×5 × 3 frames = one car per colour purple/gold/white; the parking painter tiles it; `letters` 32×8 spells CAULDRON with the 3×5 glyph font). Five extra decals not in data: `sinking`, `noAccess`, `power`, `water` (12×12 icons) and `sandbags` (16×6) — in `BSU.sprites.EXTRA_DECALS`. Anchors: data anchor `ground` → bottom-centre (`ox −w/2, oy −h + 4`); `roof`/`side` → centre. The building painter positions decals itself from `data.decals[name].anchor`.
- **Pixel font:** `BSU.sprites.FONT` (3×5, A–Z 0–9 Σ Β Υ ! - . / + ×) and `P.glyph/P.text`, plus `BSU.sprites.glyph(ctx, ch, x, y, color, zoom)` — the buildings brief's `glyph` helper lives here; reuse, do not redefine.
- **Anchors of the terrain-side sprites** (render/chunk baker): `tile:*`, `water`, `worn`, `canal` 64×32 at `(−32, −16)`; `surf:*` 64×48 at `(−32, −24)` (8 rows above for signs/railings, 8 below for piers/posts); `levee` 64×48 at `(−32, −28)` (berm rises 12 px); `floodwall` 64×54 at `(−32, −36)` (wall 18 px); `preservePost` 64×36 at `(−32, −20)`; `cliff` 32×(h+16): frame 0 = the SOUTH face (hangs under the tile's lower-left edge, `ox −32, oy 0` from the tile centre), frame 1 = the EAST face (lower-right edge, `ox 0, oy 0`), variant = face height in px (`round(Δelev × 6)`, clamped 6…96); `reeds` (−6, −13) and `knees` (−6, −4) are anchored at the tuft base; `mound` (−12, −20). All are "draw at the tile's diamond centre" — the baker adds `ox/oy`.
- **Surface variants:** `variant = mask (N1 E2 S4 W8 of same-surface 4-neighbours) | 16 bridge | 32 sign | 64 culvert`. Mask 0 draws N+S stubs (ghost preview). `canal`: `mask | 16 culvert | 32 gate | 64 gate closed` (culvert pipes also appear automatically at junctions with ≥ 3 arms). `levee`: `mask | 16 cracked`. `preservePost`: mask of NON-preserve neighbours.
- **`selfTest` under the harness:** `get('nope:thing')` is probed with a private quiet flag so the required `BSU.error` for unknown ids is not raised inside selfTest (`test/modules.mjs` fails a module whose `BSU.errors` grows). The building/entity assertions (§6 items 1, 2, 7 of the brief) run only once those painters are registered; until then the notes say "building/entity painters not loaded: their checks skipped".
- **Missing from params:** none needed beyond `params.palette`/`params.render`; the brief-named extra hexes (concrete `#B8B8BC`, steel `#8A8A8E`/`#6A6A70`, tarp blue `#3B6FD6`, skin/hair/shirt tables, …) live in `BSU.sprites.extra`.
- **Memory:** 251 canvases after `init` (1.8 MB at 1×, ~30 ms in Chrome); `gc(frameNo)` drops lazily built entries unused for 600 frames only above `CANVAS_LIMIT_MB` (8). `render` should call `sprites.gc(frameNo)` every ~300 frames as the brief says.
- **Tier 2 done:** `restoreToMarsh(state, i)` (elev → ≤ 1.0, `hydro.markRestored` when present — if hydro leaves bit 3 set terrain clears it, since otherwise the restoration is a no-op — WETLAND_ORIGINAL set, RESTORING cleared, `touch(i,'elev')`); `addWestStub(state)` lays 7 road tiles from the west edge on the land row nearest the map's middle and stores them in the lazily-initialized saved key `plot.westHighway` (the GDD gives no position for the second stub). Tier 2 left: template aesthetics only.

## economy.js

- **Shape:** `'use strict'` IIFE extending `BSU.economy` with `init/reset/tick/selfTest` plus the full ARCHITECTURE §5.7 API and the brief's additions (`addAttrition`, `buildingValue`, `happyTerms`, `timerValue`, `consumeTimer`, `setSuspendCapPenalties`, `setSuppressCoverage`). Every cross-module read goes through `M._deps.<module>` (default `BSU.<module>`), every emit through `M._deps.emit`; a missing module or function answers with a documented default (no throw), a throwing one is routed to `BSU.error('economy', '<module>.<fn>')`. Test hooks (pure, safe to ignore): `M._prestigeTarget(state)`, `M._happinessRaw(state)`, `M._projection()`.
- **Dates are derived from `BSU.dayParts(state.calendar.day)`**, not from `weather.isDate` or `calendar.month/dom`, so the module works in the brief's §7 isolation harness (which only bumps `calendar.day`) and stays consistent with the frozen contract; weather must agree with `dayParts` (ARCHITECTURE §3.7 already requires that). `flags` from `weather.tick` drive the daily/monthly steps; when `tick(state)` is called without flags the module falls back to detecting a day change on `calendar.day`.
- **Lazily initialized saved keys in `state.economy`** (both `reset` cases; structural save picks them up): `attritionBonus`, `pendingDisaster` (`null | {day, bill}`), `admittedSinceLock`, `cancelledSemester`, `cancelledUntilDay` (−1 none: the day "Semester Cancelled" lapses — the next semester start), `runningCost` (pump/generator running cost accumulated daily, posted under `upkeep` on the 1st), `blackoutDays` (consecutive days any complete building has `blackout`; > 3 → +1 blight), `lowPrestigeDays`, `probationSemesters`, `probationCardDay`, `bankruptcyCardDay`. All are finite numbers/booleans/null; never `Infinity`.
- **Festival income is booked by date** (Feb 6 Mardi Gras, Apr 5 Crawfish Boil, Oct 7 Homecoming) rather than on `festival:start`, so it cannot double-post and works without weather; the `festival:*` events are not subscribed. Listeners (owner `'economy'`): `storm:report` (queued → insurance payout + `pendingDisaster`) and `sky:phase` (DUSK → `agents.classAttendance()` pushed into `attendanceCycles` in the next tick). `game:final`, `milestone:earned`, `building:complete` need no handler (sports/progress post through `post`/`bump`; po'boys are read monthly).
- **Aug 5 order:** fall lock first, then the tuition installment, so the wave pays on arrival (this is what makes the GDD's $4.5M fall figure come out). May 5: graduation (Year 2+) → attrition → rolling round → semester-end probation seat check. `enroll:round/lock` are emitted only when `admitted > 0`; `enroll:attrition/graduation` always on their dates (count may be 0). Both `voiceApplicants` and `biblicalApplicants` are consumed at each lock (Jan 10, Aug 5), then `progress.offerBoard(state)` is called.
- **Prestige target worked number:** the brief's 31.96 assumes `academicQuality = 40`, i.e. a neutral attendance factor (`0.9 + 0.2 × 0.5 = 1.0`). With the documented default (`classAttendanceMean = 1` when no cycle was recorded) the same inputs give 32.84. `selfTest` asserts both (31.96 with `attendanceCycles = [0.5]`, 32.84 at full attendance). ## hydro.js

- **Shape:** `BSU.hydro` with `init/reset/tick/selfTest` plus every query/mutator of ARCH §5.3 and the brief (`seedInitial, depthAt, surfaceAt, stageAt, floodedBuildings, floodedTiles, predictRisk, riskAt, footprintDepth, markCanal, markPond, markLeveeChange, setJammed, networks, networkAt, surgeControl, surgeReached, surgeFrontDistance, forceRain, forceSat, setStages, conservationCheck, drainsTo, markRestored, markRestoring, stormLog, stepCount, activeCount, riverAdjacent, spillway`) and the brief's added queries `pumpRunning(state, id)`, `isMud(state, i)`. All closure state lives in one per-root context rebuilt when the root changes (D38), so `terrain.gen → seedInitial` and `rewalk → surfaceAt/stageAt` before `reset` are safe.
- **Step order inside `_step`:** rain intake → gate/barrier evaluation + heads → overtop/contact scan → 2 Jacobi passes (boundary reset after each) → pumps and ponds → active-set rebuild → **drain / evaporation / sat decay** → class crossings (rewalk/touch) → conservation. Drain runs *after* the passes so a rain step's intake can run off downhill before the ground infiltrates it; with the GDD's per-day drain rates (Dry 0.4, Wet 0.15) a 2-inch rain never pools on flat ground of any class — pooling is runoff concentration into low tiles, exactly as §3.3 says ("floods in a 2-inch cell if it is a low tile"). Weather implementers: nothing pools from a shower or a frontal rain outside real depressions; the cove works because the ridge slopes into it.
- **Stability clamp (deviation, documented in the file header):** a demanded flow is capped at half the head difference. The GDD's `(ΔH)/2 × k` with `k = 0.35` is always below that; with the canal `×8` it is 1.4·ΔH and oscillates. Canals therefore equalize at the maximum stable Jacobi rate (0.5·ΔH per pass vs 0.175·ΔH on land, i.e. ~3× faster per pass, 8× in conductance terms is not reachable by an explicit scheme). Expect a 20-tile canal to fill/drain over ~50 steps (12 s at 1×).
- **Overtopping event source:** a neighbour counts only when it is water, or a *non-levee* tile with `depth > 0`; water standing on an adjacent levee crown (rain) never overtops "its own wall" (ARCH §12.2 step 4 promises no overtop from puddles). The test uses the levee's full static crest (integrity-scaled, the open-gate rule ignored) so an open floodgate is not "overtopped" by every drop that passes through it.
- **Surge contact reset:** at `surgeControl('begin')` (per storm) and in `setStages(0, 0)` when no surge is active (per Spring High Water window). `cat` = `storms.current.cat`, else 1 (§6.9: −5 once per window).
- **Floodgate stage** = the largest live stage (surge included) of any water tile 4-adjacent to the gate's network; a landlocked network uses `max(riverStage, bayouStage)`. `gate:closed/opened` fire from `_step`, `setStages`, `surgeControl('end')` and `reset` (silently there).
- **Barrier:** read live from `buildings.list(state, 'surge_barrier')[0]` + `footprint`; its `plot.bayou` index = the smallest index among its tiles; closes when any of its bayou tiles' live stage (surge included) `> 2`, `heldStage = bayouStage` at closing, `k = 0` across all its tiles; `gate:*` payload `i` = its first footprint tile; it also calls `buildings.dirtyRing`.
- **Networks:** `{id, tiles, drainsToWater, drainsTo: 'Bayou'|'River'|'', waterAdj: number[], pumps, gates, stage}` — two informational keys beyond the contract (`drainsTo`, `waterAdj`, `stage`); readers that only use the documented four are unaffected. Pumps are matched through `buildings.list(state, 'pump')` edge tiles at network rebuild (dirty on `building:placed/removed/complete` and on `tile:changed{flags|surface|elev|crest}`).
- **Rain:** `weather.consumeRainStep` is called exactly once per hydro step with `dt > 0`; the footprint is Chebyshev `≤ radius` from `(cx, cy)` unless `mapWide`. `forceRain` keeps a private schedule applied on top of weather's (debug only).
- **Prediction:** `predictRisk` includes pumps and ponds (scratch copies of `pondCap` and gate state), no surge, stages as set; the returned `Float32Array` is 0 on water tiles; cached in `state.hydro.risk = {depth, validUntilDay, dirty}` (SAVE_SKIP strips it). Any `tile:changed` or building event marks it dirty.
- **Tier 2 done:** drained-marsh reversion (20 wet days, no canal within 2 / powered pump within 8), Marsh Restoration progress (`markRestoring` → 30 days → `terrain.restoreToMarsh(state, i)` if it exists, else `markRestored` + `classify`; `markRestored` emits `marsh:restored` and is idempotent), Bonnet Roux Spillway (`spillway(state) → {ok, tiles}`: `spillwayOffset = 1` felt by OPEN_WATER tiles with `ty < 40`, cleared when `setStages(0, 0)`; `depth += 1` once on Marsh/Wet tiles with `ty > 44`).

- **Mover anchors:** agents/gators/vehicles use float tile coords where the tile centre is `n + 0.5`, while `BSU.worldToScreen(tx, ty)` is the centre of the integer tile; movers project as `wx = (ax − ay)·32, wy = (ax + ay − 1)·16 − elev·6` and sort with `(ax − .5, ay − .5)`, so a student on tile (6,5) sorts after a building anchored at (5,5). Particles from `particles.forEachWorld(x, y, z, size, colorIdx, type)` sort by `y/16` (world px), draw as `fillRect(size·zoom)` with `colorIdx` = index into `render.particleColors[16]` (render_fx may replace the table) or a CSS colour string.
- **Chunk bake decisions:** elevations are rounded to whole px (`round(elev·6)`) before positioning — fractional `drawImage` positions left hairline seams between tiles at every zoom. Faces: a pixel step ≥ 6 px (1 ft) draws the `cliff` sprite (frame 0 = S face under the lower-left edge, 1 = E face), a step of 1–5 px draws a dark terrace band of exactly that height in a per-type colour (`stepFace`), so no background ever shows between neighbouring tiles and the Marsh's half-foot shoulders read as soft terraces instead of a brown grid. The faint `water` highlight sprite is always baked at alpha .35 on water-flag tiles; pass 3 adds the moving lines at 1×/2×. `reeds` 25 % of Marsh tiles (hashed offset), `knees` on wet tiles 4-adjacent to a cypress, `decal:sandbags` on sandbagged levee tiles, `decal:debris` on DEBRIS tiles. `tile:changed` on a chunk-edge tile also dirties the adjacent chunk in that direction (neighbour faces/masks live there). Chunks draw in `cx + cy` ascending so overhanging high tiles of the next chunk overdraw correctly. First frame after `reset`/`save:loaded`: bakes under a 150 ms budget (all 64 at 0.5× take ~120 ms in Chrome), then ≤ 2 per frame.
- **Water pass:** per visible tile `fillDiamond` from fillRect runs (1-px rows at 1×, 2-px rows at 0.5×/2×), colour `mix(waterShallow, waterDeep, depth/4)` from a 16-step cache, alpha `.55 + .3·depth/2`; water-flag tiles with `depth < .25` (ungenerated/unseeded trees) use depth 1. ## smoothing pass

Josh's playtest note: "the paths turn very jaggedly; they connect at angles. Make turns smooth and rounded and flowing better. Including stairs when paths change elevation." Before, every path/road/boardwalk tile was a `surf:<kind>` stamp picked by its 4-neighbour mask, so corners were hard right angles and a diagonal drag was a staircase of stamps. Real-Chrome tour (`smooth_tour.mjs` in the scratchpad; `smooth-NN-*.png`, 1×/2× day and night plus 3× crops): seed 42, an L path that becomes a T, a diagonal staircase, a loop, a road wiggle, a boardwalk snaking over marsh and bayou, a path climbing the west hill's cliffs both ways, a dorm with tees on both visible faces. Zero console errors / uncaught exceptions.

- **render.js, section "Smoothing pass" (before `bakeChunk`)** — `traceNets(surf, w, h)` builds the 4-connected network of each surface kind 1–3 and cuts it into polylines between nodes (dead ends, junctions, isolated tiles; a direction-bit `used` mask so every edge is walked once) plus closed loops; `smoothPoly` is Chaikin ×3 (open polylines keep both endpoints, so junction centres never move; a corner passes 0.18 tile inside the corner tile's centre, within every width's half, so agents walking tile centres stay on the surface; a unit zigzag becomes a wave of ±0.18); `buildCurves(state)` smooths every polyline once per change (`curvesDirty` from `tile:changed`, `building:complete/removed`, `reset`, `save:loaded`; 0.1 ms for the whole map) and buckets each smoothed segment by the tiles its (width-padded) bbox touches, so the bake asks "which pieces touch tile i" in O(1). `elevRuns` splits a polyline's tiles into equal-elevation runs; each run boundary becomes a step record keyed by the LOWER tile with the exact crossing point of the curve on the shared edge (`nearestOnCurve` projection) and the walk direction.
- **Per-tile stroking inside the tx+ty loop (`drawCurvesAt`)** — the pieces are stroked under `setTransform(32, 16, −32, 16, −ch.ox, −ep − ch.oy)` (ground units → chunk px at THAT tile's elevation) with round joins/caps, clipped to the tile's diamond (`clipTile`, a device-space hexagon stretched up 9 px for bridge rails / down 5–14 px for posts and piers). Drawing per tile rather than per run keeps the painter's order right: a raised neighbour drawn later still overdraws the curve. The clip grows `CLIP_EPS` = 0.06 tile toward the S and E neighbours only (the ones drawn later), so the later tile's ground overdraws the overlap and no anti-aliased conflation seam shows on shared edges or chunk borders (checked at 3×: loop columns across y = 15|16, the T arm across x = 39|40). Widths in tiles: path 0.42 (dark edge, gravel, lighter centre, two dashed speckle strokes), road 0.72 (curb, asphalt, gold centre dashes `[0.14, 0.14]`), boardwalk 0.5 (dark rails, deck, plank gaps as a wide dashed stroke so the ticks run across the curve, bark posts every other smoothed point on the viewer-side edge). Isolated tiles are 0.24-long pills. Dead ends get round caps. `tile:changed` now also re-bakes the neighbouring chunk when the tile is ONE in from the chunk edge (a Chaikin ×3 point depends on four originals).
- **Stairs / ramps (`drawStep`)** — drawn on the lower tile's side from the shared edge at the higher level down along the curve, after that tile's stroke, unclipped: `d` px of step ≥ 3 → stairs with `nT = d < 5 ? 2 : clamp(round(d/3), 3, 6)` treads of run `min(0.15, 0.6/nT)` tile (lighter tread quads, darker riser quads, a 1-px nosing line, 1×6 bark handrail posts at both ends on both sides), stone (`creamStone` shades) for paths and wood for boardwalks; 1–2 px steps get a 0.1-tile wedge in the surface's own darker tone (a stone wedge read as a crosswalk bar and chopped the diagonal, `smooth-03` of the first run). **Roads get a ramp, not stairs**: cars cannot take steps, so a sloped asphalt quad with its curbs and three gold hatch lines (`L = min(0.5, 0.12 + 0.012·d)`). When the lower tile lies toward the viewer (S/E) the flight is painted top → bottom and reads as a staircase down the visible face (`smooth-11`); when it lies behind (N/W) the higher tile's ground, drawn later, hides all but the lower treads peeking past its back edge (`smooth-12`), which is what the camera would see.

## time pass

Josh after playing: "the default speed of the time movement is still too high." 1× was 20 s per calendar day (a 40-minute year). Now **1× = 40 s per day, 400 s per month, 80 minutes per year**, and nothing else got slower.

**Mechanism chosen: `params.time.ticksPerDay` 100 → 200** (the plan's preferred option), not `baseTps` 5 → 3. The calendar slows; the world keeps its 5 ticks/s, so agents (2 tiles/s on a path, 1.2 on grass), vehicles, water (2 hydro steps/s), every animation, the undo window (50 ticks = 10 s), the sky cycle (300 ticks = 60 s, now 1.5 days per cycle instead of 3) and every set piece (900 / 750 / 250 / 150 / 300 ticks at 10 ticks/s) are exactly as lively as before. `baseTps` would have made all of them 40–60 % sluggish. The audit found every per-day rate already derived from `ticksPerDay` or fixable in a handful of edits; no hydro acceptance band moved.

**Single source of truth.** `contract.js` now defines `TICKS_PER_DAY = 200`, `DAY_SCALE = TICKS_PER_DAY / 100` and `HYDRO_STEPS_PER_DAY = TICKS_PER_DAY × 4 / 10` (80: hydro steps on ticks 0/2/5/7 of each 10-tick block) before `BSU.params`, and derives from them: `time.ticksPerYear` (24,000), `time.skyCycleDays` (1.5), `time.dayScale`, `hydro.stepsPerDay`, `hydro.dtDayNormal` (1/80), `rain.mapWideSteps` (80: a shower / frontal / band is one calendar day of steps), `rain.cellSteps` (120 = 1.5 days), `rain.cellTicks`, `rain.cellDriftTicks` (20: a cell still drifts ~15 tiles over its life), `riskSteps` (80), `agents.wearDecay` (10). **Never write a literal 100, 40 or 1/40 for ticks-per-day or steps-per-day again.**
